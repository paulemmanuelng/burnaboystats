### Decisions locked (Paul)

- **Certified units, never "units sold."** A plaque is a FLOOR.
- Features: toggle, **default off**.
- Nigeria: **separated, never deleted**. Opens ON for **57 of 120 pairs** —
  `(both artists >=50% Nigerian) OR (either side has zero international plaques)`.
  A third clause for thin-but-not-empty pairs is **provably redundant**: 0 pairs.
- Song select shows cover + name + artist names; artist select shows both images.
- Compare button on **every** artist's certifications page, stat card moves to
  secondary rather than being removed.
- All 27 countries researched before launch. No partial ship.
- **Any pair, no home team.** Not Burna vs a challenger.

### The four engine rules, each with a live counter-example

1. **Never sum a release's own awards.** Zero duplicates in the data today, so it
   never fires — but `certHistory` is an append-only event log and anything built
   off that would roughly double every figure.
2. **A multiplier rides whatever tier it sits on.** Tyla's "Water" is 2x DIAMOND
   in Brazil, the only such case in 1,212 plaques.
3. **What cannot be priced is counted and named.** 35 plaques sit in a
   country/format with no usable threshold.
4. **An award PROGRAMME overrides the country's scale.** RIAA Latin certifies a
   Platino at 60,000 against the standard programme's 1,000,000. **This was a real
   bug I shipped and caught:** the first engine ignored `cert.body` and priced
   "Dai Dai"'s US 2x Platino at 2,000,000 instead of 120,000.
