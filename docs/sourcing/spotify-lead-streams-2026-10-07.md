## What ChartMasters' "lead" is, and is not

ChartMasters does not file every song by Spotify's credit role. Its public song table for Burna Boy (the top 20) puts "Location", "Own It", "WE PRAY" and "Loved By You" in its "Features" group, although Spotify's "View credits" panel lists Burna Boy as a Main Artist on all four. Under Spotify's credits his split is 9.86B lead / 1.17B featured on kworb's 6 Oct page (`credit-roles-2026-10-07.md`, which also has the board's twenty and the cause of each difference). ChartMasters also re-files songs from week to week: Tyla read 4.7b / 136.8m on 30 Sep and 4.4b / 486.8m on 7 Oct, after "Show Me Love" (349M) moved into its Features group.

The board's list keeps ChartMasters' own figures because it compares twenty artists on one source. The site has read every track's Spotify credits for Burna Boy only; the board's own reads cover 52–96% of each artist's streams. /music prints Burna Boy's split by credit role and ChartMasters' figures as a dated cross-check.
