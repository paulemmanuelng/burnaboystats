# IFPI — International Certification Award levels, updated June 2013 (the Greek rows)

Read 20 Sep 2026 from the Wayback capture of IFPI's own PDF:

    https://web.archive.org/web/20140328112251id_/http://ifpi.org/content/library/international-award-levels.pdf

7 pages; the file's own header reads "International Certification Award levels — Updated June 2013". The Greece rows, verbatim from the extracted text (page number in brackets):

- [p.1] `Greece 6,000 12,000 – 3,000 6,000 –`
- [p.4] `Greece 3,000 6,000 –`
- [p.6] `Greece 3,000 6,000 –`

Column order on the albums table is "Domestic repertoire: Gold Platinum Diamond | International repertoire: Gold Platinum Diamond"; on the singles and music-video tables it is "Gold Platinum Diamond". So:

| format | Gold | Platinum | Diamond |
|---|---|---|---|
| Albums — domestic repertoire | 6,000 | 12,000 | — |
| Albums — international repertoire | 3,000 | 6,000 | — |
| Singles | 3,000 | 6,000 | — |
| Music videos | 3,000 | 6,000 | — |

"Unit sales required" — physical-era shipments; no streaming ratio existed in 2013. This is the last figure IFPI or IFPI Greece ever published for Greek award levels (ifpi.gr today carries none — docs/sourcing/CERT-THRESHOLDS.md, 11 Sep 2026). Every artist on this site is international repertoire in Greece, so the international-repertoire album column is the one that applies.

Applied 20 Sep 2026 on Paul's decision: Greek plaques are priced at these levels and flagged as a historic level on every line, rather than listed-and-never-summed.
