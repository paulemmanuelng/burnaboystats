# Lead or featured: Rule C, the way ChartMasters files it (read 7 Oct 2026)

## The rule

Paul, 7 Oct 2026: lead vs featured must read "exactly as ChartMasters reads it". He approved Rule C and three overrides.

A song is **LEAD** for an artist when either:

1. **Own release.** The same song is on any release in the artist's **own** Spotify discography: albums, singles & EPs, and compilations where they are a release artist. Matching is by **title**, never by track id, because kworb's track id is often another copy of the song. Burna Boy's "Dai Dai" on kworb is not the copy on his own "Dai Dai" single, and kworb's "wgft" is the copy on Gunna's album. The title is normalised first: lower case, curly quotes made straight, zero-width characters stripped, and a "(feat./ft./with …)" or "[with …]" bracket, a " - feat. …" tail and " - Single Version" dropped (`scripts/roles/rule-c-lib.mjs`, `normTitle`).
2. **First-listed.** The artist is the first-listed artist on the track: kworb's song page shows no "*" against it.

Everything else is **FEATURED**.

- **Overrides** (`app/data/roleOverrides.json`). Three songs sit on the artist's own single, so Rule C makes them lead, but ChartMasters files them under the artist's "Features". Paul approved filing them featured: Tyla "Show Me Love (with Tyla)", Ayra Starr "Overloading (OVERDOSE)" and Black Sherif "Come & Go - Black Sherif Remix".
- **Not on Spotify with the artist.** The release billing decides: "X ft. ARTIST" is featured, anything else is lead. A release whose title is in the artist's own discography IS on Spotify with them, even when no kworb track matched. Olamide's "Currently" (Kizz Daniel ft. Falz, Olamide & LK Kuddy) is in Olamide's discography, so it is his lead.
- **Title collisions.** Part 1 matches by title, so a DIFFERENT song that only shares the title would wrongly count. Such a row carries `titleCollision` (the reason, with the other record's track ids) in the inputs, and part 1 is skipped for it, so the first-listed test decides. There is one: Davido's "For You" on the site is Teni ft. Davido (2021, kworb 4c7UBMrX7NC9QHtZQCQKBn, Davido starred), while the "For You" on his own *Omo Baba Olowo: The Genesis* and *Best Of Davido* is his 2012 solo song (4Y8tHAxEeNN9icB7tojrgj / 0zQliE5cjhG5ZnY0sSP1DJ). It is his feature. The review of 7 Oct 2026 checked every other part-1 match: each is the same record.
- **Albums** keep their own group.
- **One record, two roles.** Each artist gets their own role. "Ginger" is Wizkid's lead (it is on his album) and Burna Boy's feature. "Like" (Iyanya ft. Davido & Kizz Daniel) is Davido's lead and Kizz Daniel's feature.

Examples (Burna Boy): **lead**: "Dai Dai", "WGFT" (his own single "wgft (feat. Burna Boy)"), "My Oasis", "Play Play", "Tshwala Bam (Remix)", and "Alone" (no release of his own, but he is the only artist on the track). **Featured**: "Location", "Own It" (only the Toddla T remix single is in his discography, and that title is kept distinct), "Be Honest", "We Pray", "Loved by You" and "Ginger".

Burna Boy's **co-leads** are his leads whose billing is not his own ("Burna Boy" / "Burna Boy ft. X"). There are 20, and every one is lead by part 1. The names on the tag are stored (`coLeadWith`) and never parsed from a credit line.

## Filing a NEW release (refresh)

1. Find the song's Spotify title and whether the artist is first-listed: on their kworb song page, no "*" against it.
2. Run `node scripts/roles/rule-c-role.mjs <slug> "<Spotify title>" [--first-listed]`. It prints LEAD or FEATURED and why. When it says LEAD because the title is on one of the artist's own releases, check that release carries the SAME song; if it is a different record with the same title, add `titleCollision` to the row (step 3). **If the song came out after the artist's `readOn` in `own-releases-2026-10-07.json`, re-read their discography first:** open open.spotify.com/artist/&lt;id&gt;/discography/all in list view (read-only), record every release's id, title, type line and track titles, and replace that artist's block with today's `readOn`.
3. Add the release to `rule-c-inputs-2026-10-07.json` (`spotifyId`, `spotifyTitle`, `firstListed`, and `billing` / `coLeadWith` / `titleCollision` where they apply). Run `node scripts/roles/build-song-roles.mjs`, then file the ledger row the way it says: Burna Boy's `singles`/`features` and `singleCharts`/`featureCharts`, the board's `kind`. `tests/songRoles.test.ts` fails until the ledgers and the roles agree.
4. A song ChartMasters files differently from Rule C goes to Paul. An override is a ruling, never a default.

## What it replaced

Rule C replaces the credit-role filing of 6–7 Oct 2026, which used Spotify's "View credits" role (Main Artist = lead). That rule put Burna Boy at 9.86B lead against ChartMasters' 8.0B, because ChartMasters files "Location", "Own It", "WE PRAY" and "Loved By You" as features although Spotify credits him as a main artist on each. The raw credit reads stay in ~/burnaboy-work/roles (not committed).
