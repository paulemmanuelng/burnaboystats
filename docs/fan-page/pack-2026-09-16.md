## House rules for the page's copy

- Numbers as the site prints them; never round up, never "over", never "sold".
- Say where the number comes from (the body, the chart) — that is the site's whole point, and the reason the page can post it with confidence.
- No claims the site does not carry (e.g. worldwide units sold). "Most-streamed African artist of 2026" is carried from 17 Sep: Burna Boy 1,810,927,983 through 16 Sep, 2.7 million past Wizkid — say "so far", and re-read the board on the day, the gap is a day's streams.
- Link the page the figure lives on, not just the home page.
