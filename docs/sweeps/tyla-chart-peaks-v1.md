# Tyla — every official chart peak (chart-body sweep + A–Z country sweep, audit-repaired)

**Standard:** identical to burnaboystats.com's `charts.ts` inclusion rules — **one entry per country per release**, on that country's **principal national singles or albums chart**. Singles and albums both count, recorded separately. **Lead, co-billed and featured credits all count.** Where a country runs two or more principal charts (Belgium's two language sides; the Netherlands' Single Top 100 and Nederlandse Top 40; Japan's Billboard Japan and Oricon; Malaysia's, the Philippines' and Thailand's IFPI and Billboard charts) the **better** peak is reported as that country's single entry. **Billboard Global 200 and Billboard Global Excl. U.S. count as their own two entries** (🌐 and 🌍).

**Airplay carve-out:** an airplay chart counts **only** where a country publishes no non-airplay national chart. Here that admits 🇧🇬 Bulgaria (PROPHON), 🇪🇪 Estonia, 🇰🇿 Kazakhstan, 🇧🇾 Belarus, 🇲🇩 Moldova, 🇷🇺 Russia and 🇺🇦 Ukraine (TopHit Top Radio Hits), 🇬🇹 Guatemala and 🇵🇦 Panama (Monitor Latino / PRODUCE), 🇱🇧 Lebanon (OLT20), 🇲🇹 Malta, 🇲🇰 North Macedonia, 🇷🇸 Serbia and 🇸🇮 Slovenia (Radiomonitor), and 🇻🇪 Venezuela (Record Report). Where a country runs **both**, the non-airplay chart wins every time — which is why Poland, Spain, Croatia, Romania, Finland, Slovakia, Nigeria and — **new in this repair** — 🇹🇷 Türkiye are recorded on their sales/streaming charts and their better airplay peaks are thrown away.

**Excluded everywhere:** platform charts (Spotify, Apple Music, iTunes, Deezer, Shazam, YouTube, Anghami, Audiomack, Boomplay) — never official, however widely quoted; genre charts (Afrobeats, Hip-Hop/R&B, Dance, World); component charts (streaming, sales, download, physical, vinyl, video, airplay sub-charts, repertoire and language sub-lists sitting under a combined national chart); extension charts (US Bubbling Under, NZ Hot Singles, Japan Hot Overseas, UK 101–200, Ultratip, NL Single Tip, MAHASZ Editors' Choice, Monitor Latino "Anglo" lists); and supranational aggregates other than the two Billboard Global charts (IFPI MENA, Official North Africa).

Original sweep **17 Aug 2026**; **audit repair pass applied the same day** — see *Audit repairs (17 Aug 2026)* at the foot of the file. This file merges the earlier regional chart-body sweep with a full A–Z country sweep, then applies the repair pass, which removed 21 entries and re-verified a further 8 at source.

‡ = the chart body was reached but the specific week carrying the peak could not be read; the figure rests on a chart-table citation and is **not** promoted to verified. Exactly one entry carries it (Iceland).
