# Ayra Starr — every official chart peak (full A–Z chart-body sweep)

**Standard:** identical to burnaboystats.com. One entry per **country**, on that country's **principal national singles or albums chart** (Billboard Hot 100 / Billboard 200, OCC, IRMA, SNEP, GfK, ARIA, RMNZ, Ultratop, Hung Medien mirrors, FIMI, PROMUSICAE, AFP/Audiogest, TurnTable Top 100, TOSAC, VG-lista, Sverigetopplistan, Hitlisten, MAHASZ, ZPAV/OLiS, Circle Chart, and Billboard's national Songs charts where that is a country's main chart). **Both singles and albums count**, recorded separately. **Lead and featured credits both count.** *peak* = the highest position the release has ever reached on that chart. Where a country runs two principal charts (Belgium's two language sides, the Netherlands' Single Top 100 and Nederlandse Top 40, Japan's Billboard/Oricon pair, the Philippines' three, Brazil's Billboard/Pro-Música pair) the **better** peak is reported as that country's single entry. **Billboard Global 200 and Global 200 Excl. US count as their own two entries.**

**Airplay carve-out:** an airplay chart counts **only** where a country publishes no non-airplay national chart — Bulgaria (PROPHON), Lebanon (OLT20), Malta and North Macedonia and Serbia and Slovenia (Radiomonitor), Russia/Ukraine/Kazakhstan/Moldova/Estonia/Belarus (TopHit), Panama (PRODUCE), and the Monitor Latino markets. Where a country runs **both**, the non-airplay chart wins every time — so Poland, Spain, Nigeria, Croatia, South Africa, Germany, Mexico, Romania **and Türkiye** all have better airplay peaks that are **not** used.

**Excluded throughout:** platform charts (Spotify, Apple Music, iTunes, Deezer, Shazam, YouTube, Audiomack, Boomplay — never official, however widely quoted); genre and component charts (Afrobeats, Hip-Hop/R&B, Latin, Streaming/Sales/Downloads/Video/Physical sub-charts, repertoire splits); extension charts sitting below a main chart (US Bubbling Under, NZ Hot Singles, NL Single Tip/Tipparade, UK 101–200, Ultratip, ARIA Hitseekers, Sverigetopplistan Heatseeker); and supranational regional charts (Official MENA Chart, Official North Africa Chart, Central America + Caribbean).
