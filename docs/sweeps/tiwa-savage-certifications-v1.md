# Tiwa Savage — every certification (body-side sweep)

**Method:** identical to the rest of this series — a certification counts when it
appears in the **issuing body's own register**, never in a press release, a fan
tally or a wiki. One plaque per title per country at its **current** tier (a 2×
Platinum is one plaque, not two; an upgrade replaces, it does not add).
**Lead, co-billed and featured credits all count**; writer and producer credits
do not. Singles and albums are recorded separately. A register read in full and
holding nothing is a **proven zero**; a register that could not be read is a
**gap**, never a zero. Swept **24 Sep 2026** (find pass), and every row re-read
at its register by a separate verifier the same day.

Tiwa Savage is the Lagos-born singer (Tiwatope Savage). TCSN prints her as
`Tiwa Savage` on every one of her 25 raw rows, and its 2023 row for *Loaded*
carries the label `222 Ent., Everything Savage, EMPIRE`. Every register was
searched for "Tiwa", never for a bare "Savage": that surname belongs to other
acts in every register read — 21 Savage, Savage Garden, the New Zealand rapper
Savage (`Timmy Trumpet & Savage`, `Savage feat. Aaradhna`) and, inside TCSN
itself, the Nigerian singer Savage (`Savage & BNXN | Confident`, `Savage &
Victony | Rosemary`, both counted on BNXN's and Victony's boards; TurnTable's own
chart printed that record as `Confident | Savage & Buju` in July 2021). None of
those is hers.

## Total: **12 certifications**

**Country split:** NG + NZ = 11 + 1 = **12** ✓

**Tier split:** Platinum + Gold + Silver = 6 + 2 + 4 = **12** ✓ (one of the six Platinums is 2×; the two Golds are Nigeria's *Somebody's Son* and New Zealand's *Romantic*; all four Silvers are Nigerian)

All 12 are **singles**. No register read holds an album or EP of hers — not
*Once Upon a Time*, *R.E.D*, *Sugarcane*, *Celia*, *Water & Garri* or *This One
Is Personal* — and TCSN files every row it holds as a single.

**Seven of the twelve are features.** Five are her own lead singles (*Jaiye
Foreign*, *Loaded*, *Stamina*, *Somebody's Son*, *Pick Up*); the rest are
records led by Spyro, 1da Banton, Ruger, Korede Bello, ODUMODUBLVCK, Majeeed and
Bella Shmurda, each with her name in the register's own credit.

## Priced on /compare, and linked on /methodology
