# Davido (David Adedeji Adeleke) — every official chart peak (full A–Z world sweep)

**Standard:** identical to burnaboystats.com. **Count** one entry per country per release, taken from that country's **principal national singles or albums chart** — Billboard Hot 100 / Billboard 200, Official Charts Company, IRMA, SNEP, GfK/BVMI, Ultratop, Hung Medien national charts, FIMI, PROMUSICAE, TurnTable Official Nigeria Top 100, Nationale Top 40 Suriname, and Billboard's national *Songs* charts where that is a country's main chart. Both singles and albums count, recorded separately, and **lead, joint and featured credits all count**. Where a country runs two main charts (Belgium's two language sides; the Netherlands' Single Top 100 and Top 40; Japan's Billboard/Oricon pair) the **better** peak is reported as that country's one entry. **Billboard Global 200 and Global 200 Excl. US count as their own two entries.** `peak` = the highest position the release ever reached on that chart.

**Airplay carve-out:** an airplay chart counts only where a country publishes **no** non-airplay national chart — Bulgaria (PROPHON), the Monitor Latino markets, Türkiye (Radiomonitor), Serbia, Slovenia, North Macedonia, Malta, Lebanon, Panama, Venezuela, and the TopHit markets (Russia, Ukraine, Estonia, Belarus, Kazakhstan and Moldova — all six confirmed readable this session). Where a country runs **both**, the non-airplay chart wins every time — Poland, Spain, Croatia and **Nigeria** all have better airplay charts that are **not** used. The carve-out is not engaged anywhere in Davido's data: every one of his 13 chart countries has a non-airplay principal chart, and he is a directly-read zero in all six TopHit territories.

**Excluded:** genre and component charts (US Afrobeats Songs, UK Official Afrobeats Chart, UK Sales / Streaming / Downloads / Physical / Video Streaming, Latin Airplay, repertoire "Anglo" splits, Portugal's two AFP streaming sections, Korea's Circle Download / Streaming / BGM / V Coloring / Retail Album charts); extension charts (UK 101–200, the OCC mid-week **Official Singles Chart Update**, NZ Hot Singles, Ultratip, NL Single Tip, US Bubbling Under, TurnTable Recurrents); **platform charts** (Spotify, Apple Music, iTunes, Deezer, Shazam, YouTube, Audiomack, Boomplay) however widely quoted; supranational regional charts (Official MENA Chart, Official North Africa Chart); and year-end aggregates.

**Sweep date: 17 August 2026.** This file merges the earlier regional sweep (16 Aug 2026) with the full A–Z country sweep, and then applies the **17 Aug audit repairs** — a verification pass that closed twelve open source holes and reversed one bad promotion. See *Audit repairs (17 Aug 2026)* at the foot of the file.

### Nigeria: what "verified" can and cannot mean

Thirty-five of the 66 entries are Nigerian, so the provenance of the TurnTable data is load-bearing and is stated exactly.

TurnTable's site is a Next.js app whose chart payload is fetched at `/_next/data/<buildId>/charts/1.json` (Official Nigeria Top 100) and `/charts/2.json` (Official Top 100 Albums). Both were pulled directly on 17 Aug 2026 and return the **6 Aug 2026** chart: exactly 100 singles rows and 100 album rows, each carrying a `highestPosition` field. **The endpoint keys on the chart *category* id, not the weekly instance id, and serves only the most recent week** — weekly instance ids (5726, 5725, 5700) all return HTTP 404, while category ids resolve normally. TurnTable publishes no weekly archive.

The consequence is structural, not a coverage failure that can be fixed by trying harder: **the API can only ever verify a release that happens to be on the chart at read time.** For Davido that is 18 of his 35 Nigerian entries — 13 singles (B4 B4, I Know Who I Be, Gimme Dat Ting, Already Falling, Tell Everybody, On the Road, Amazing Grace, Yaya, My Light, Constantly, Julie, Guide, Zanzibar) and all 5 albums. Every one of those 18 peaks matched the value already on file **exactly**; nothing had to be changed.

The other **17 Nigerian singles** — the historic No. 1s and the 2020–2025 catalogue — are off the current chart and rest on TurnTable's editorial write-ups. They are marked `°` above. They are not disputed and are not being downgraded in value; they are simply unverifiable at source by any route TurnTable exposes, and the file now says so rather than implying the API backed the whole Nigerian set.

**Open peaks.** Ten *Oriade* album tracks (Already Falling #9, Tell Everybody #16, On the Road #19, Amazing Grace #20, Yaya #23, My Light #24, Constantly #25, Julie #28, Guide #30, Zanzibar #35) were in **week 1** on the 6 Aug 2026 chart, so their `highestPosition` equals their debut rank and their peaks are still open. *Gimme Dat Ting* (#5, 3 weeks) may also still improve. These eleven need a re-read in a later week. *B4 B4* and *Oriade* are also week-1 rows but are already at No. 1 and cannot improve.

### Context

Under this identical standard, **Burna Boy** holds **280 chart entries** and **48 No. 1 placements** across **71 chart countries** (burnaboystats.com). Davido's **66 entries** and **17 No. 1 placements** across **13 countries** sit well below that, and the honest reading is that the gap is one of geography rather than of scale at home.

- **At home they are comparable.** Sixteen of Davido's 17 No. 1 placements are Nigerian, 13 of them TurnTable No. 1 singles — a haul very few artists anywhere match, including several multi-week and debut-at-No. 1 runs, plus three No. 1 albums and a 175-week chart life for *Timeless*.
- **Abroad the gap is the whole story.** Davido charts in 13 countries; Burna Boy in 71. Davido has **zero** entries in Germany, Austria, all five Nordic countries, all nine Central/Eastern European countries swept, all six TopHit airplay markets, Australia, New Zealand, all six Asian territories, all six MENA territories, and all twelve Latin American airplay markets. After the repair pass most of those zeroes are proven against the chart bodies' own complete archives rather than merely unfound. Burna Boy charts in most of them.
- **No Global 200 entry at all** is the single sharpest contrast. Davido has never placed on Billboard's global charts; that is where an artist's international reach shows up, and his is blank.
- **The UK is his real second market, but a thinner one than the A–Z sweep suggested.** Nine entries — five singles and four albums — ahead of any other single country outside Nigeria, but fewer than the US, Ireland, France, the Netherlands, Switzerland, Belgium, Italy, Spain, Canada and Argentina combined (18). His UK singles presence is genuinely small: five Official Singles Chart entries, one of them a Tion Wayne lead and one a Chris Brown lead.

Albums are also where his UK presence is real — four of his nine UK entries are albums, and three of the four charted top 10 or top 100 on the strength of one week each.
- **One non-Nigerian No. 1, and it is a World Cup song.** *Hayya Hayya (Better Together)* topping the Nationale Top 40 Suriname is the only time a Davido credit has led a national chart outside Nigeria.

### 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates — closed, and every UAE figure independently confirmed
The Official UAE Chart was swept in full through the same open IFPI/BMAT API that settled Saudi Arabia and Egypt (`api.theofficialmenachart.com/v1`, region key `ae`): **3,020 chart rows across all 194 published weeks, 2022–2026**. Every UAE peak already in this file was reproduced independently by that sweep, so UAE entries no longer rest on a citation. Confirmed No. 1s: *Raindance* (Dave & Tems), *Dai Dai* (Shakira & Burna Boy) and *Water* (Tyla). Also read: *Chanel* #3, *Calm Down* #3, *Push 2 Start* #16, *WE PRAY* #8. Davido and Ayra Starr have **no UAE placement** — a proven zero, read rather than unfound.

### 🇮🇸 Iceland — the disagreement resolved, one peak upgraded, one still short of proof
The two sweeps disagreed because they were looking at different things. `tonlistinn.is` is genuinely dead and unrelated to the chart; Tónlistinn's real home is FHF's **plotutidindi.is**, which is live. It publishes the current week and the year-end lists only, so a historical weekly peak has to come from archived captures of the weekly page — there are **105** of them, 2016–2026.

- ***Wait For U*** (Future ft. Drake & Tems) 🇮🇸 **#11 — now verified.** Read directly in the archived weekly Top 40 of 16 July 2022, credited "Future, Tems". The figure was right all along; it simply had no proof attached until now.
- ***Calm Down*** (Rema) 🇮🇸 **#31 stands.** A second capture (4 Feb 2024) shows #37; the earlier Dec 2023 read of #31 is the better peak and is retained.
- ***Water*** (Tyla) 🇮🇸 **#14 remains unproven** and keeps its marker. Only 8 weekly captures survive for 2024 and none covers the week the peak would fall in. Absence from those eight is not disproof — the figure is neither confirmed nor refuted, and is left flagged rather than promoted or deleted.

**A correction worth recording:** the year-end lists are *not* peaks. The 2016 year-end Top 100 places "One Dance" at #18 credited to "Drake" alone, which is the likeliest origin of a claimed Icelandic peak of 18 for that record. A year-end ranking and a weekly peak are different measurements, and only the weekly one satisfies this file's standard.
