# CKay — certification sweep v1

**Swept 26 August 2026.** Chukwuka Ekweani, Nigeria. Twelfth artist on the
Afrobeats Board.

Counted by the board's rule: **one plaque per title per country at its current
tier**. A 5× Platinum is one plaque, not five. Lead and featured credits both
count; writer and producer credits do not. Every plaque below was read in an
issuing body's own register.

## Total: **27 certifications**

Across **14 countries**, on **10 titles**.

## The shape of it

Fourteen of the twenty-seven belong to one record. "love nwantiti (ah ah ah)"
is certified in fourteen countries — the widest single-record spread on the
board — and it reaches that spread through versions the registers name
differently: the original, the ElGrande Toto North African remix, the Joeboy &
Kuami Eugene remix, the Franglish French remix, the Pronto & Eunique German
remix. **They are one record and are counted once per country.** Counting the
remixes separately is the single easiest way to inflate this artist's total,
and several fan tallies do exactly that.

**France certified two of his records Diamond** — "love nwantiti" and
"Emiliana". No other country has given him anything above Platinum, and France
gave him two.

**Featured credits, counted as the rules require:** "La La" is Davido's record
and "Beggie Beggie" is Ayra Starr's. Both carry his credit, so both count. The
shared cover art is what revealed the second one — it wears Ayra Starr's
"19 & Dangerous" sleeve, which is how a mis-filed lead credit was caught.

## What could NOT be read — gaps, not absences

These are ceilings on completeness. None is evidence that a plaque does not
exist, and none was counted.

- **Ireland (IRMA)** publishes no certification register at all. Not a failed
  page load — there is nothing to read.
- **Sweden (IFPI Sverige)** likewise publishes no public register.
- **Switzerland** — hitparade.ch's Edelmetall register would not load on any
  path tried.
- **Belgium (BEA/Ultratop)** sits behind a Cloudflare interactive challenge.
- **Nigeria (TCSN)** — the live register caps at 500 rows and serves no Silver
  tier at all, so the Nigerian count is a floor. The two Silvers above come
  from elsewhere in the sweep. See `project-tcsn-register-provenance`.

A later pass with access to any of these can only move the total UP.
