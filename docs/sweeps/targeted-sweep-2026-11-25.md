## "On the Low": 2021, not 2026

Audiogest's reply of 23 Nov 2026 said "On the Low: Gold (2 June 2026)", and the
file logged the day as 2026-06-02. Today's full list prints the same day and
month with the year 2021: "1 | Gold | ON THE LOW | BURNA BOY | 02/06/2021".

The full list wins for three reasons:
- It is a table with an issue-date column, the body's own record.
- Its old-style capitals match the other 2020–2021 rows ("JERUSALEMA" 2021, "OWN IT" 2020), while every 2024–2026 row is in mixed case.
- The reply's other two dates agree with the table, so the 2026 was most likely a typing slip.

The level does not change. Its units on /compare do not change either, because
plaques are priced at today's thresholds. The plaque does move from the 2026
by-year log to 2021, so the 2026 international count drops by one.
