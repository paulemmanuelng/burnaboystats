## Known gaps

Read as: an unread register is a gap, not a zero.

1. **🇿🇦 RiSA's live table is currently missing roughly its last twelve months.** It ends at **09 Sep 2025** and holds 608 rows today. The sibling sweeps of 12–17 Aug 2026 read **748** rows from this same host including a 29 May 2026 row (Dave & Tems — Raindance, Platinum). Today `?title=Raindance` returns nothing and `?artist=Tems` returns only Essence (31 Aug 2023). **~140 rows covering Sep 2025 → mid-2026 are not being served.** Any 2026 South African plaque — *Isaka (6AM)* with Ciza is the obvious candidate — would be invisible. **The South African count is a floor.**
2. **🇳🇬 Six TCSN Wayback captures are permanently unreadable** — 20230929234834, 20231129022850, 20240226113805, 20240529152014, 20241204155343, 20250214052135. Each returns HTTP 200 truncated at exactly 1,048,576 bytes, reproducibly. All six sit inside runs of same-era captures with identical row counts (103-row and 206-row eras), so the union is very unlikely to be affected — but they are unread and are not being called clean. **40 of 46 captures were read.**
3. **🇳🇬 TCSN publishes no per-plaque award date.** All 500 live rows read 2026-02-06; the 2,477-row capture uses six dates across the whole register. These are **bulk batch/migration dates**. No certification in this sweep can be dated to the day it was awarded — the dates printed in the tables above are the *foreign* registers' own award dates only.
4. **🇳🇬 TCSN certification is request/label-based** (see the project's TCSN provenance note). Absence of a title proves no application was filed and audited — it proves nothing about streams. The same caveat applies to RiSA and IFPI Norge.
5. **🇳🇬 RCN (2021) is unverifiable.** It awarded 92 music-video certifications before going dormant, and Omah Lay was active and huge in exactly that window (Get Layd, What Have We Done). If he holds RCN plaques, **no surviving register can confirm them.** Unverifiable, therefore uncounted.
6. **🇬🇷 Greece — PARTIAL, NOT A ZERO.** IFPI Greece publishes certifications only as an unlabelled "Award" column inside its weekly Digital Singles (International) chart, and ifpi.gr no longer serves charts at all — the page must be reconstructed from Wayback. **66 archived captures were read** (2022:10, 2023:10, 2024:17, 2025:26, 2026:3) out of roughly 250 published weeks; the Award column and the Tems control both appear, and no Omah Lay row was found in any capture read. The Internet Archive rate-limited the crawl. **~three-quarters of Greek weeks are UNREAD. Do not record Greece as zero.**
7. **🇯🇵 Japan (RIAJ) — NOT READ.** `riaj.or.jp/data/gd/search/` renders results client-side and would not return rows even for a Bieber control. No result either way.
8. **🇳🇱 Netherlands caveat.** nvpi.nl itself is refusing connections (ECONNREFUSED 37.48.108.47:443, from two independent network paths). The Dutch **result stands** — the register was read at goudplatina.nl, which NVPI's own pages point to — but the NVPI-branded `/ajax/gpl` endpoint named in the standing access notes is currently unreachable.
9. **🇫🇮 Finland is "not covered", not zero.** The certification year listings stop at 2015, so a Finnish plaque could not show even if awarded.
10. **Ten registers were not attempted** (Czechia, Slovakia, Turkey, Croatia, Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Peru, Singapore, Malaysia, South Korea). Nine were shown by earlier sweeps in this series to publish no usable register; none is counted here either way.

***soso* is the only release certified in more than three countries.** It holds 7 plaques at six different tiers simultaneously — 🇫🇷 Diamant, 🇳🇬 6× Platinum, 🇵🇹 Platina, 🇨🇦 Platinum, 🇺🇸 Gold, 🇪🇸 Oro, 🇬🇧 Silver — which is a clean illustration of how far national thresholds diverge for one identical record.

**Four finds that exist only in a register.** None is on the artist-side lists fans compile, and each was invisible until the issuing body's own database was opened: 🇩🇪 *Another Vibe* (Luciano feat. Omah Lay, Gold 2025) — his only German plaque, and it is a feature on a German rapper's record; 🇿🇦 *Forever (Remix)* (Gyakie feat. Omah Lay, Platinum 28.07.2022); 🇬🇧 Silver for Olamide's *Infinity*, certified **07.08.2026 — twelve days before this sweep**; and 🇵🇹 *soso* Platina, which exists in the public record only as the two letters `PL` in the `Gal.` column of one weekly Top 200 PDF. **Had that week not been captured, Portugal would read as a zero.**

### Context

Under the same rules: **Burna Boy 230 certifications across 26 countries** ([burnaboystats.com](https://burnaboystats.com)). Omah Lay's **61 across 9 countries** is **27% of Burna Boy's plaque count and 35% of his country reach**.

The two lines are not measuring the same career. Burna Boy's total is spread across 26 markets; Omah Lay's is a Nigerian catalogue with a French annex. His Nigerian depth is genuinely large — **43 plaques on 40 distinct titles**, including a 6× Platinum, a 5×, three 4×s and four 3×s — and it is the deepest single-market ledger in this sweep set outside Burna Boy's own. What he does not yet have is breadth: **eight countries outside Nigeria**, against Burna Boy's 25, and **fourteen national registers read end-to-end return nothing**. The Nigerian figure is also the one most likely to be a floor, because TCSN is request-based and its live page publishes less than half of what its own archive holds.
