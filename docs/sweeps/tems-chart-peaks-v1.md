# Tems — every official chart peak (worldwide A–Z sweep)

**Standard:** identical to burnaboystats.com. One entry per **country** per **release**, on that country's **principal national singles or albums chart**. **Singles and albums both count, recorded separately. Lead, co-billed and featured credits all count.** *peak* = the highest position the release has ever reached on that chart. **Billboard Global 200 (🌍) and Billboard Global Excl. U.S. (🌐) count as their own two entries.** Where a country runs two co-principal charts — Belgium's two language sides, the Netherlands' Single Top 100 and Top 40, Greece's Local/International split, Malaysia's and the Philippines' two national charts, Japan's Billboard/Oricon pair — the **better** peak is reported as that country's single entry.

**Airplay carve-out:** an airplay chart counts **only** where a country publishes no non-airplay national chart. Where a country runs both, the non-airplay chart wins every time — which is why Poland, Spain, Nigeria, Croatia and (from this pass) **Türkiye** are all recorded off their sales/streaming charts despite better airplay peaks existing.

**Excluded everywhere:** platform charts (Spotify, Apple Music, iTunes, Deezer, Shazam, YouTube, Audiomack, Boomplay) — never official, however widely quoted; genre charts (Billboard U.S. Afrobeats Songs, Hot R&B/Hip-Hop, Hot Rap, World, UK/NZ Afrobeats); component charts (Streaming Songs, Radio Songs, Sales, Video Streaming, TopHit "All Media", Monitor Latino "Anglo", RIM "International", Circle Retail Album, AFP "Top 200 Stream Albuns" and "Top 200 Streams", Scottish sales breakouts); extension charts sitting below a main chart (US Bubbling Under, NZ Hot Singles, ARIA Hitseekers, Swedish Heatseeker, Ultratip, NL Single Tip, UK positions 101–200, TurnTable Recurrents); and supranational regional charts (IFPI MENA, IFPI North Africa).

Sweep date **17 Aug 2026**, repaired **17 Aug 2026**. `°` = the peak rests on a dated citation to the chart body rather than a live read at source, because that body publishes only the current week or blocked access this session; everything unmarked was read in the chart body's own data or its Hung Medien mirror.
