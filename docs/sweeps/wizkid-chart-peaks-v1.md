# Wizkid — Official Chart Peak History

**Standard:** one entry per country, on that country's **principal national singles or albums chart**. **Both singles and albums count**, recorded separately, and **lead and featured credits both count**. Where a country runs two main charts (Belgium's two language sides; the Netherlands' Single Top 100 and Nederlandse Top 40; Japan's Billboard Japan and Oricon), the **better peak** is reported as that country's single entry. **Billboard Global 200** and **Billboard Global 200 Excl. US** count as their own two entries. *Peak* = the highest position the release has ever reached on that chart. **Airplay carve-out:** an airplay chart counts only where a country publishes **no** non-airplay national chart — Bulgaria (PROPHON), the Monitor Latino countries, Türkiye (Radiomonitor), TopHit for Ukraine, Kazakhstan, Moldova and Belarus, TopHit Russia, Lebanon (OLT20), Malta, Serbia, Slovenia, North Macedonia, Estonia, Panama (PRODUCE), Venezuela (Record Report). Where a country runs **both**, the non-airplay chart wins every time — Poland, Spain, Nigeria and Croatia all have better airplay peaks that are **not** used. **Excluded everywhere:** genre and component charts (Afrobeats, Hip-Hop/R&B, Streaming Songs, Radio Songs, Digital Song Sales, Monitor Latino "Anglo" lists); extension charts sitting below a main chart (US Bubbling Under, NZ Hot Singles, NL Single Tip, Ultratip, UK 101–200, Sverigetopplistan Heatseeker, TurnTable Recurrents); and **platform charts** (Spotify, Apple Music, iTunes, Deezer, Shazam, YouTube, Audiomack, Boomplay) — never official, however widely quoted. Year-end and annual rankings are not peaks.

**Sweep date: 17 August 2026.** Repaired 17 August 2026 — see *Audit repairs* below.

Global-chart shorthand in the tables: 🌐 = Billboard Global 200, 🌍 = Billboard Global 200 Excl. US. **Both Billboard Global charts launched with the issue dated 19 September 2020; no release that peaked before that date can carry a Global row.**

### Context

Under the **identical standard**, Burna Boy has **280 chart entries** and **48 No. 1 placements** across **71 chart countries** ([burnaboystats.com](https://burnaboystats.com)).

| | Wizkid | Burna Boy |
|---|---:|---:|

Burna Boy is ahead on all three, and the gap in **country breadth** (32 vs 71) is wider than the gap in entries — Burna Boy charts in roughly twice as many territories, which is what compounds into nearly twice the entry count. Wizkid's footprint is concentrated: Nigeria (24 entries), the UK (23) and the US/Global charts (19) supply 66 of 154 entries, **43%** of his total. He returns zero across East, South and Southeast Asia, zero on all three MENA national charts (Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the UAE — the first two now proven by full reads of their entire published history), and zero on every Billboard Latin American national chart.

Two qualifications that cut in Wizkid's favour, and one that does not:

- **His single biggest record is bigger.** "One Dance" alone accounts for 25 entries and 15 of his 24 No. 1 placements, including No. 1 in the US, UK, Australia, Canada, Germany, France and the Netherlands. That is a spread of chart-topping markets, and no comparison should read Wizkid's lower totals as an absence of top-tier hits.
- **Featured-credit dependence.** 15 of those 24 No. 1s come from one Drake feature, and his No. 1s outside Nigeria are *entirely* that one song. As a lead artist his best US peak is No. 9 ("Essence") and his best Global 200 peak is No. 28.
- **Coverage is not the explanation for the gap — and this is now demonstrated, not argued.** The four charts previously listed as unreachable have all been opened. Three returned proven zeroes (Belarus, Kazakhstan, Egypt) and the fourth confirmed a figure already held (Moldova #15). What remains genuinely unread — Poland's OLiS at source, ARIA positions 51–100, the pre-Dec-2022 Suriname archive, and TOSAC's own site — are markets in which Wizkid has few or no known placements. Closing every one of them would move the total by single digits, not by 126.

- ***Wait For U*** (Future ft. Drake & Tems) 🇮🇸 **#11 — now verified.** Read directly in the archived weekly Top 40 of 16 July 2022, credited "Future, Tems". The figure was right all along; it simply had no proof attached until now.

**A correction worth recording:** the year-end lists are *not* peaks. A year-end ranking and a weekly peak are different measurements, and only the weekly one satisfies this file's standard.
