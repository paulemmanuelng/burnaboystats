# CKay — official chart peaks v1

**Swept 26 August 2026.** Counted by the board's rule: **one entry per title
per country**, on that country's principal national chart, at its best peak.
No platform charts (Spotify, Apple, iTunes, Deezer). No genre or format charts.
Airplay only where a country publishes nothing else. Billboard's two worldwide
charts are recorded separately as GLB and GLBX, not as countries.

## The five No. 1s

Switzerland, France, the Netherlands and Norway all took "love nwantiti" to
No. 1. The fifth is Nigerian and is a different record entirely: **"BODY (danz)"
topped TurnTable's Official Nigeria Top 100** — a placement the widely-copied
fan tallies miss, because they list "Emiliana" at No. 5 as his Nigerian best.

## One record, many names

Registers and charts name the versions differently — the original, the
ElGrande Toto North African remix, the Joeboy & Kuami Eugene remix. Germany,
Austria, Denmark and Italy charted the North African remix; the Netherlands,
Switzerland and Norway the Joeboy & Kuami Eugene version. **All are the same
record and are counted once per country, at the best peak any version reached.**

## What could NOT be read — gaps, not absences

Not counted, and not disproved:

- **Billboard** (Hot 100, Billboard 200, Global 200, Canadian Hot 100) is
  behind a TollBit paywall that serves a chart-less shell to every access path.
  Only Global Excl. US was recovered. Widely-reported figures exist for US #26,
  Canada #5 and Global 200 #2 — none is published here because none could be
  read at source.
- **Portugal (AFP)** — a No. 1 is reported and could not be confirmed at the
  body. If it holds, it is a sixth No. 1.
- **Ireland, Greece, Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary** — reported entries, none
  confirmable at source within this sweep.
- **South Africa (TOSAC)** — reported at #2, not readable.
- **Finland** returned a clean nil: the chart loaded and CKay has no entry.

Every one of these can only move the total UP.
