### ČNS IFPI: the chart-row badge IS the register

Every earlier sweep document recorded Czechia and Slovakia as "no public
register". That was wrong, and it is corrected in each artist's file. ČNS IFPI
publishes no award list, but the badge printed in each row of its weekly
Singles Digital Top 100 ("Zlatý singl" / "Platinový singl") is the award: across
111,400 rows it is only ever those two strings, the two award names in the
rules; no pre-2022 title (the rules' eligibility floor) ever carries one; and a
title's badge can differ between the two charts in the same week (*Calm Down*
week 31/2023: CZ Zlatý, SK Platinový). It is the route the site already used
for "Dai Dai" CZ Gold and SK Platinum, both still current in week 38/2026. The
limit is structural: a title awarded while off the Top 100 never shows, and the
albums charts carry no badge at all.
