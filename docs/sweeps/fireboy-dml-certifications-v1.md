# Fireboy DML — every certification (body-side sweep)

**Method:** identical to the rest of this series — a certification counts when it appears in the **issuing body's own register**, never in a press release, a fan tally or a wiki. One plaque per title per country at its **current** tier (a 3× Platinum is one plaque, not three; an upgrade replaces, it does not add). **Lead, co-billed and featured credits all count**; writer and producer credits do not. A register read in full and holding nothing is a **proven zero**; a register that could not be read is a **gap**, never a zero. Swept **21 Aug 2026**.

## Nigeria — how it was read

TurnTable's live register serves a hard cap of 500 rows and **no Silver at all**, so it shows only part of what it has issued. The Nigerian rows above are the **union of every archived capture** of `turntablecharts.com/certification` — `__NEXT_DATA__` → `props.pageProps.certEntries` — taking the highest tier ever published per record. Two traps were applied: rows are keyed on **(title, artiste)**, because a title alone merges genuinely different records; and artist-string variants of the same record are then folded, because the same release is filed under more than one spelling.

**Gaps** — could not be read, and must never be quoted as zeros: 🇮🇪 IRMA — the register moved off irma.ie and then closed, so its 838 rows cannot answer for artists of this era. 🇬🇷 IFPI Greece — not reached.

## Honesty note

Every international row was verified **twice** against the issuing body's own register, and once more by hand afterwards. The New Zealand rows are read from RMNZ's register as published through **RadioScope** (a TablePress table, 24,196 single rows), not from recordedmusic.co.nz, which carries no certification register at all.
