# Seyi Vibez — every official chart peak (full chart-body sweep)

**Standard:** identical to burnaboystats.com. One entry per **release per country**, on that country's **principal national singles or albums chart**. **Both singles and albums count**, recorded separately. **Lead, co-billed and featured credits all count.** *peak* = the highest position the release has ever reached on that chart. **Billboard Global 200 and Global 200 Excl. US count as their own two entries** (codes GLB / GLBX). An **airplay** chart counts only where a country publishes no non-airplay national chart.

**Excluded throughout:** platform charts (Spotify, Apple, iTunes, Deezer, Shazam, YouTube, Audiomack, Boomplay); genre and component charts (Afrobeats, Street-POP, Afro-POP, Hip-Hop/Rap, Streaming Songs, Radio Songs, repertoire splits); extension charts sitting below a main chart (US Bubbling Under, NZ Hot Singles, UK 101–200, TurnTable Bubbling Under); year-end and mid-week charts; supranational regional charts (IFPI MENA, IFPI North Africa); and certifications. Sweep 19 Aug 2026.

**One row was removed after an explicit attempt to refute it, and it changes the headline from 115 to 114.**

- 🇳🇬 ***Billion Dollar* — No. 10, claimed with a 20-week run. Removed.** The No. 10 peak is **not readable in the issuing body's register**, and what the register does show contradicts the row as written. TurnTable's Official Nigeria Top 100 series begins at issue **2022-07-07** — every entry on that issue carries `weeksOnChart` 1, and the 2022-09-22 issue tops out at 12 weeks, confirming it as week one of the series. On that first issue the payload carries **`Billion Dolla` / `Seyi Vibez` at rank 89**, `highestPosition` 89, `weeksOnChart` 1. No title `Billion Dollar` appears in any Official Nigeria Top 100 issue between 2022-07-07 and 2026-08-13, and the "20 weekly issues" figure is contradicted by the register, which shows a single week.
  The claimed No. 10 belongs to the **pre-July-2022 TurnTable Top 50**, and that register is **not retrievable**: the archived Top 50 pages (captures of 2022-04-17, 2022-05-05, 2022-05-21, 2022-06-10) are Nuxt SSR shells whose state object carries `charts:[]` — the rows were fetched client-side from a backend that is now dead and was never archived. **The May 2022 Top 50 is therefore a genuine gap, not a zero**, and No. 10 must not be published as a figure.
  The only register-readable peak for this title is **No. 89**, and that figure did not survive verification as a published claim either, so **the title carries no chart row in this file at all**. It is not a zero — it is unread.
  ⚠️ *Billion Dollar*'s **certification** (🇳🇬 Platinum, TCSN) is a separate register read and is unaffected. See `seyi-vibez-certifications-v1.md`. A refuted chart peak does not invalidate a verified plaque.

## Known gaps

1. 🇿🇦 **South Africa is a zero only to the depth TOSAC itself published.** All 206 issues were read and none carries a Seyi Vibez row, but TOSAC's archive is uneven: **55 issues are 100-deep** (mostly 2022), 24 are 50-deep, 32 are 20-deep and **94 are only 10-deep** (most of 2023–2025). Independently, Billboard South Africa Songs was read **25-deep for all 308 weeks** with no Seyi Vibez row. So the combined South African zero is solid **to at least No. 25 continuously from Sep 2020, and to No. 100 only in the 55 weeks TOSAC published a Top 100.** A placing of 26–100 in a week TOSAC published only a Top 10 or Top 20 **cannot be excluded from the published record.** Also noted: one TOSAC issue (Top 50, week 14-2025, slug `…-2-2`) renders only 2 rows and looks like a duplicate stub.
2. 🇳🇬 **The pre-July-2022 TurnTable Top 50 is unrecoverable.** Its archived pages are Nuxt shells with `charts:[]`; the backend that served the rows is dead and was never captured. Any Seyi Vibez placing between his debut and 7 Jul 2022 that is not visible in the surviving payloads is **unread, not absent** — this is exactly what sank the *Billion Dollar* row above. His earliest register-readable entries, *Catalyst* (#50, 28 Jan 2021) and *90's* (#36, 29 Apr 2021), sit inside the recoverable window.
3. 🇳🇬 **The archive endpoint keys on (category, ISO week, year)** and returns the first matching instance. If TurnTable ever published two instances inside one calendar week, only one is reachable. 302 distinct singles issues across a 301-week span, and 197 album issues across a 196-week span, indicate essentially one per week — but this is a structural limit, not a verified absence.
4. **Title merges and splits, flagged rather than hidden.** *"Gangsta"* (#74) and *"Gangsta (Remix)"* (#39) are kept **separate** on differing `musicLink`s. *"Billion Dollar Baby"* (#2, 2022) and *"Billion Dollar Baby 2.0"* (#1) are kept **separate** because TurnTable charted them as separate titles with separate runs. *"FUJI MOTO"* appears twice — once as a single (#8), once as the album (#1) — which is correct, since singles and albums are counted separately. **If a reader treats the two Billion Dollar Baby editions and the two Gangsta versions as single releases, the total falls from 114 to 112 and the No. 1 count is unchanged.**
5. **Billboard's 2020 back-extension is slightly short.** The pre-career 2020 weeks returned 5,000 of an expected 5,200 Hot 100 rows and 9,400 of an expected 10,400 Billboard 200 rows — a handful of 2020 weeks did not render. **Immaterial:** Seyi Vibez's first chart appearance anywhere is 28 Jan 2021.
6. **Billboard's artist page is corroboration, not proof.** `billboard.com/artist/seyi-vibez/` renders no chart-history module and carries no `data-artist-id`, unlike `billboard.com/artist/rema/` (id 180781) — i.e. Billboard's own database holds no chart-artist record for him. That is **corroboration only**: Asake's page also lacks the id despite having charted. The weekly sweep is what carries the four Billboard zeroes: **226,200 rows** across Global 200, Global Excl. US, Hot 100 and Billboard 200 (233,900 including Billboard South Africa Songs).
7. **Two runs were still live at the sweep date** — *GTA* at No. 2 in its second week and *OPERA MINI* still at No. 60 on the 6/13 Aug 2026 charts. This file is a snapshot of a career still adding rows, and both peaks could improve.
8. **Not every chart territory on earth was individually read.** The sweep covered Nigeria, Africa, the four Billboard charts, and the rest-of-world instruments listed in the coverage table. Territories not named in that table — much of Asia, Oceania, Latin America and eastern Europe — returned nothing and were **not** individually enumerated with controls. Under the house standard they are **unread, not proven zero**, and no claim is made about them either way. Given that he has no entry in any of the 20-plus instruments that *were* read with controls, the probability of a hidden placing elsewhere is low — but low probability is not evidence.

## Methodological result worth keeping

**The Nigerian weekly archive is reachable.** `turntablecharts.com/api/ttc-proxy/api/chart/{categoryId}/{week}/{year}` — the site's own front-end proxy — returns a base64 `payload` field for **any** week, not just the current one. It is `Referer`- and `Origin`-gated and rate-limited to 100 requests/minute. Category 1 is the singles chart, category 2 the albums chart.

This **closes the "Nigeria is structurally unverifiable beyond the current week" gap** carried in the Tems, Rema and Ayra Starr files in this series. Every Nigerian peak on burnaboystats.com that currently rests on a citation marker rather than a live read can now be verified at source, for **every** artist, back to 5 Nov 2020.

### Context

Under this identical standard, **Burna Boy has 280 chart entries and 48 No. 1 placements across 71 chart territories** ([burnaboystats.com](https://burnaboystats.com)).

The comparison is not close, and it is not really a comparison of size — it is a comparison of shape. Seyi Vibez's **114 entries** is **41% of Burna Boy's count**, which for an artist whose first chart week was January 2021 is a remarkable rate. But those 114 entries sit in **1 chart territory against Burna Boy's 71**, and his **11 No. 1s against 48** are all in that one country. Burna Boy's ledger is 71 territories deep and comparatively shallow in each; Seyi Vibez's is one territory deep and, within it, denser than anyone else's in this sweep series.

What drives the density is that TurnTable charts album cuts and interludes: *Apala Interlude* at No. 7, *Fuji Interlude* at No. 13, *Highlife Interlude* at No. 46. **103 charting singles in five years in one market** is the highest single-market singles count in the series. And the catalogue does not fall off — *Thy Kingdom Come* has 163 weeks on the albums chart, *NSNV* 163, *Vibe Till Thy Kingdom Come* 151, *NAHAMciaga* 138.

The gap is entirely at the border. Burna Boy's 280 includes a Hot 100 line, a Global 200 line and a UK line; Seyi Vibez has **no entry on any chart outside Nigeria**, and this sweep read the registers rather than assuming it — 309 Global 200 weeks, 309 Global Excl. US weeks, 346 Hot 100 weeks, 346 Billboard 200 weeks, 261 FIMI weeks, 246 Portuguese weekly PDFs, 489 Czech and Slovak weekly charts, the whole NZ search index and every published MENA week. That is the finding, and it is a read result, not a silence.
