The live page serves 500 rows, the same 500 it served on 30 Sep: 456 Platinum

byte-identical to the Wayback captures of the same route on 1 Oct, 27 Oct and
5 Nov 2026 (same etag and SHA-256) and equal, row for row, to the 30 Sep
capture of the page. Three builds served it (`9FLVyEQ3OX_KP6OGJD8nk` from about
24 Sep, `aN3bZ2dLwKvK-gWUKpe7I` by 27 Oct, `J0nfKCEyPR0F3BkOP_0S3` by 5 Nov),
each with the same payload, so a stale cache is not the explanation.

**Unchanged since February, not only since 30 Sep.** The 500 rows are exactly
the first 500 rows of the uncapped 21 Feb 2026 list (Wayback
`20260221224010`, 2,477 rows), in order, on title, artiste, format, label,
`isClaimed` and tier, once "Platinum" is read as "Platinum_1" (268 rows) and
"Gold" as "Gold_1" (44). Only the stamp (2025-02-06 in the capture,
2026-02-06 live) and the ids (1361–1860 then, 1–500 now) differ.

**The sort order, and what the window can show.** The 21 Feb list sorts by
date stamp, newest first, and then by id. It is not sorted by tier: in seven
places the tier goes back up while the ids keep rising (in the 2024-10-14 and
2023-06-08 batches; for example id 231 *Giza* Platinum_2, then id 232 *Dog Eat
Dog* Platinum_3). Its newest batch, 1,274 rows (456 Platinum, 367 Gold, 451
Silver, ids 1361–2634), looks tier-sorted only because it was loaded in tier
order; its rows 501–1,274 are 323 Gold and 451 Silver. So:

- a row added under the current stamp, at any tier, Platinum included, lands
  after row 1,274 and is never shown;
- the window can show only a batch with a *later* stamp arriving on top, and
  only if the live service still sorts by stamp, which cannot be proven now
  that the ids are renumbered 1–500;
- an unchanged window is therefore not evidence that nothing was awarded. The
  line "the unchanged window rules out new Platinum awards" (the register
  lane's) does not hold and is not used.

**No album row since the launch.** All 500 live rows are singles, and the 21 Feb
rows are singles or blank. The only album row any readable state has held is
Wizkid's *Made in Lagos* Gold, in the register's two-row launch state of Feb 2023
(Wayback `20230214120546`, gone by 6 Mar 2023; `wizkid-certifications-v1.md`),
though the page now publishes album thresholds. The three album plaques
above (*Work of Art*, *Mr. Money With The Vibe*, *The Game Needs Us*) cannot be
