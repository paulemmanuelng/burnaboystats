The rule, and its exceptions

One screen, two states. ≤3 countries: the country strip collapses into the "shape of the record" panel — the concentration is stated as the finding it is. ≥4 countries: the panel yields to a best-tier country rail. The tier accordion is constant in both.

Density. One row per release. A release's plaques appear as its tier chip + market count; the full per-country list opens on the row (build behaviour). Worst case (Seyi, 103 plaques) is 3 section headers + 44 rows fully expanded — ~4.5 screens, down from 17.4.

Chrome. Back bar on top, five-tab bar at the foot — Paul's rule for the board, never two pinned bars. All targets ≥44px.

Zero is a value. A tier an artist doesn't hold renders as "0 · none held", never hidden (three artists have no international plaques at all).
