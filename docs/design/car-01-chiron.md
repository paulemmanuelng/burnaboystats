## The car

A **one-of-one** custom build by Dubai's Venuum, unveiled July 2026 and billed as the world's first widebody Chiron. Reported as the most expensive car in West Africa. This is the collection's flagship and the most photographed car in it — which makes it the right pilot: resemblance is easy to verify and impossible to fake.

- **The reveal video (primary):** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YNP3X59Yv6Y
- **Image search that returns it from every angle:** `burna boy bugatti chiron all angles` — front, rear, three-quarter and detail shots are all in the first rows. Also `burna boy bugatti chiron venuum`.

## Required features — the fidelity checklist

The gate checks every item. Numbers 1–4 are the car's identity; getting those right *is* the job.

1. **Two-tone split: baby blue over gloss black.** Light sky/baby blue on the bonnet, roof and upper body; gloss black on the lower body and rear. The split follows the Chiron's signature **C-line** — the sweeping curve behind the door. This car reads "blue at the front, black at the back."
2. **French tricolour centre stripe.** A slim blue-white-red stripe running the car's centreline — nose, bonnet, roof, down the rear deck.
3. **The "7" roundel on the bonnet.** A dark circular roundel with a gold/amber **7**, sitting on the bonnet's centreline (the stripe runs through/behind it). This is the single most recognisable mark on the car.
4. **Widebody stance.** Venuum's conversion: pronounced flared arches front and rear, wide track, low. A standard-body Chiron silhouette fails the gate.
5. **Bugatti face:** horseshoe grille at the nose, slim four-element headlights.
6. **Dark multi-spoke wheels** — black/dark grey, visible spoke pattern, not plain discs.
7. **At this angle, hint the rear:** the full-width thin rear light bar and the wide rear arch should read in the three-quarter view.

**Optional (too fine for flat vector at this size — do not force):** the name engraved on the rear spoiler, "VENUUM" script across the tail (rear-facing; barely visible at this angle), interior details.

## Suggested flats

From the references, roughly: baby blue (body upper), a darker blue shade for form (allowed as a second flat, not a gradient), gloss black (lower/rear), near-black window tint, tricolour blue/white/red, gold-amber (the 7), dark grey (wheels), soft shadow. That's eight — within budget.

## What done looks like

Someone who has seen the reveal video looks at the SVG and says *"that's Burna Boy's Chiron"* — not "that's a Bugatti." Blue-over-black split ✓, tricolour stripe ✓, 7 roundel ✓, widebody arches ✓, on the fixed angle and viewBox. First car sets the conventions for the other nineteen, so expect one or two rounds of adjustment at the fidelity gate — that's the process working, not failing.
